What to expect

Kids stay busy with toys and fun activities like coloring and games. For older kids, we also work in basic skills like writing and numbers. Everyone gets 30 minutes of outside time to play in the sun.

What to pack

  • Milk bottle
  • Water bottle
  • Change of clothes (shirt & pants)
  • Diapers, for changing
  • Extra pair of shoes
  • Lunch & snacks (optional)

Breakfast, lunch, snacks, and milk are provided daily — you're welcome to pack your own instead. Bedding is provided, but your child is welcome to bring their own if they have something they like to nap with.

If your child has allergies, please let us know and pack their own food — it makes things easier and avoids any cross-contamination, which keeps everyone safer.
